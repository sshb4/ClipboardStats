BEHIND THE CLIPBOARD

Built for the
“did you see that?”
kind of fan.

Some numbers are too good to get lost in a box score.

Clipboard Stats is an independent fan project with a simple idea: follow one player and one exciting stat. We turn stats from public APIs into focused trackers, so you can keep up with the moments you care about.

Free forever and supported by donations. No affiliation with any league, team, or player. Just fans keeping count.
